# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A 14 -year, heavy-usage subscriber who has outgrown the platform's ability to serve her (UXR-01, Priya, 34).
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** To spend her limited evening leisure time actually watching something, not searching for it.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Twenty minutes of scrolling a 15,000-title catalog ending in zero watches - the exact "massive library, no value" tension my hook names. Critically, her response wasn't to switch to a competitor's streaming app; she reverted to a physical DVD, meaning the entire category temporarily lost to a legacy format. This is the starkest evidence in the dataset that discovery failure isn't just a churn risk to competitors,  it's a retreat from the format itself.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Physical DVD (personal collection/library)
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. Opens StreamLine app to browse. 2. Scrolls the home/discovery screen for ~20 minutes. 3. Finds nothing worth watching. 4. Closes the app without watching anything. 5. Retrieves a physical DVD instead.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The moment the 20-minute search ends in zero watches. The catalog's scale (15,000 titles) produces no usable result, making the search itself pure wasted effort rather than a tradeoff.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** "I open the app, scroll for like twenty minutes, and close it without watching anything. There's 15,000 titles but nothing I actually want. I ended up going back to a DVD." (UXR-01, Priya, 34)
